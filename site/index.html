in Apple Home.

A Homebridge plugin that puts your Hitachi air conditioners in the Home app, with frost wash, quick mode, humidity and air quality. It works with the AirCloud Home app from Hitachi Taiwan.

Install in Homebridge View on GitHub

Free and open source. Works with Homebridge 1.6 or later, including 2.x.

Through the AirCloud Home cloud

Hitachi's Wi-Fi air conditioners are controlled through the AirCloud Home cloud, so the plugin signs in there with your account, just like the app. The cloud pushes every change to Homebridge as it happens, and if it goes down for maintenance the plugin reconnects on its own.

What you get in the Home app

Each air conditioner shows up as one accessory, with everything the plugin can control grouped together.

  • Power, mode and temperature

    Cool, heat and auto, within the temperature range your unit reports, in 1° steps.

  • Fan speed

    Seven steps on the slider: auto, silent, low, medium, high, rapid and express.

  • Humidity and air quality

    Indoor humidity and a PM2.5 air quality sensor, from the unit's own readings. Hide them on any unit that doesn't need them.

  • Frost wash and quick mode

    A notification when a frost wash is due, switches for frost wash and quick mode, and an automatic wash.

  • Always up to date

    Changes made with the remote or the AirCloud Home app appear in the Home app within seconds.

  • Siri, scenes and automations

    Units work like any other Home accessory, so you can ask Siri or cool the bedroom before you get home.

Frost wash, without having to remember New in 1.5

Hitachi units with frost wash (凍結洗淨) freeze and thaw the heat exchanger to rinse off dust, and tell you when it's time. The plugin turns that reminder into a sensor the Home app can notify you about, and adds a switch to start a wash.

With automatic frost wash, you don't have to act on the reminder at all: the wash starts by itself after you switch the unit off.

How automatic frost wash works

Each air conditioner gets its own 自動凍結洗淨 switch, so you choose which units wash themselves.

  1. Turn on 自動凍結洗淨

    Flip the switch on the air conditioner in the Home app.

  2. Switch the unit off as usual

    From the Home app, the remote or the AirCloud Home app. Nothing happens yet.

  3. Five minutes later

    If the unit is still off and asks for a wash, the plugin starts it. Turn the unit back on before then and it won't wash.

The frost wash, quick mode and 自動凍結洗淨 switches need Homebridge to be signed in with the unit's owner account.

For Hitachi air conditioners on AirCloud Home

Every supported unit on your account shows up automatically. There's nothing to enter per device.

Air conditioners
Single and multi-split units registered in the AirCloud Home app.
Owner or shared account
A separate account with the units shared to it is the most reliable. The frost wash and quick mode switches need the owner account.
Dehumidifiers and air purifiers
Recognized on the account, but not shown in HomeKit yet.
Only what you need
In the plugin settings, switch a unit off to keep it out of HomeKit, or hide just its frost wash, humidity or air quality items.

Not supported: AirCloud Go

AirCloud Go is a different Hitachi app with its own cloud service. Units registered only there don't appear in this plugin.

Set it up in a few minutes

  1. Install the plugin

    In the Homebridge UI, open Plugins, search for Jci Hitachi Platform and install it.

  2. Sign in

    Open the plugin settings and enter your AirCloud Home email and password. The device list shows every unit on the account, and the page is in English, Traditional Chinese or Japanese, following your Homebridge UI language.

  3. Restart Homebridge

    Your air conditioners appear in the Home app, on the bridge you already use.

Requires Homebridge 1.6 or later (2.x supported) and Node.js 18, 20, 22 or 24. Full configuration reference
